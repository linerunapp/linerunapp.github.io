Line Run Access

Free for students, schools and community theatres

Learning lines is part of learning the craft. If you’re a drama student or in a community theatre production, Line Run Access gives you unlimited scripts and scenes, free.

What you get

  • Unlimited scripts and scenes
  • Record the other parts in your own voice
  • Rehearse out loud: Line Run listens and moves the scene on as you speak

Importing a script and the generated voices stay part of Line Run Pro.

Who can apply

  • Students aged 13 and over in high school, college, university or a theatre training program, on your own
  • Actors in a community theatre production
  • Teachers, directors and anyone running a school or community production, for your whole cast

Under 13? Ask a parent or teacher to apply for you. Working professionally? Line Run Pro is made for you.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below. It takes about two minutes.
  2. You’ll see a confirmation as soon as you send it.
  3. I read every application myself and reply from hello at linerunapp dot com, usually within a few days. Check your junk folder too.
  4. In the app, go to Settings, then Redeem a Code.
